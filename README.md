# Phoenix Tech Solutions

Phoenix Tech Solutions is a student‑led initiative based in Alpharetta, Georgia. We are part of the FCS Innovation Academy, and our core mission is to empower nonprofits and charitable organizations with practical, sustainable, and cost‑effective technology solutions that amplify their outreach and impact.

## 🎯 Mission

- **Empowerment through Technology**: Provide tailored software and digital tools so organizations can focus on their mission rather than technical hurdles.
- **Student‑Driven Innovation**: Leverage the energy and creativity of our student team to deliver modern, scalable solutions.
- **Community Collaboration**: Partner closely with nonprofit leaders to ensure our work aligns with real needs and measurable outcomes.

## 👥 Our Team

We are a multidisciplinary group of high‑school students specializing in software development, AI/ML, UX/UI design, and project management. Our collective goal is to bridge the technical gap for organizations with limited resources.
